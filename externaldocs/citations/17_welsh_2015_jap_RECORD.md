## Role in the programme

Primary human anchor for the Wave 2 gradual-erosion design (`scenarios_erosion/`). Welsh et
al. study an actor's **own** behaviour: small transgressions increase the likelihood of
larger later transgressions. Gino & Bazerman 2009 (entry 08) study **observers'** acceptance
of others' gradual erosion, so it is the secondary anchor here. Evidence class:
LAB_EXPERIMENT (four studies per the abstract; not read in full here — verify the design
details before citing specifics).
