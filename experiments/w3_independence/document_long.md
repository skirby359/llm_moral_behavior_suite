# MASTER SERVICES AND DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

**Between:** Northgate Analytics LLC ("Provider") and Kestrel Manufacturing Inc. ("Customer")

This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and governs all Services provided
by Provider to Customer.

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Agreement" means this document together with Appendix A and Appendix B.

1.2 "Services" means the services described in Appendix A.

1.3 "Confidential Information" means information that is confidential and that is
disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement.

1.4 "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable
natural person.

1.5 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday
observed in the Territory.

1.6 "Security Incident" means any unauthorized access to, or disclosure of,
Personal Data held by Provider.

1.7 "Permitted Subprocessor" means a third party engaged by Provider to process
Personal Data, which has been approved in writing by Customer.

1.8 "Fees" means the amounts payable by Customer under Section 3.

---

## 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 Provider shall perform the Services listed in Appendix A in accordance with
this Agreement.

2.2 Provider may modify the manner of performance provided the modification does
not reduce the functionality available to Customer.

2.3 Customer shall provide Provider with such access to Customer systems as is
reasonably necessary for performance of the Services.

2.4 Any change to the scope of the Services must be agreed in writing in accordance
with the change control procedure set out in Section 9.

2.5 Provider shall not be responsible for delays caused by Customer's failure to
provide access under Section 2.3.

---

## 3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Customer shall pay the Fees in accordance with this Section 3.

3.2 The monthly Fee for the Services is $4,750, payable in advance.

3.3 The annual Fee, if Customer elects annual prepayment, is $58,000, representing
twelve monthly payments.

3.4 Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

3.5 Invoices in excess of $10,000 are payable within forty-five (45) days of the
invoice date.

3.6 The thirty (30) day payment term in Section 3.4 applies to all invoices without
exception.

3.7 Provider may charge interest on overdue amounts at 1.5% per month.

---

## 4. DATA PROTECTION

4.1 Provider shall process Personal Data only as necessary to provide the Services
and in accordance with Customer's written instructions.

4.2 Provider shall not transfer Restricted Data outside the Territory without
Customer's prior written consent.

4.3 Provider shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to
protect Personal Data.

4.4 The Data Steward shall maintain a record of all processing activities and make
it available to Customer on request.

4.5 Provider shall notify Customer of any Security Incident within seventy-two (72)
hours of becoming aware of it.

4.6 The technical and organizational measures referred to in Section 4.3 are set out
in Appendix C.

4.7 Notification under Section 4.5 shall be made within three (3) Business Days of
Provider becoming aware of the Security Incident.

4.8 Provider shall engage subprocessors only with Customer's prior written consent.

---

## 5. SERVICE LEVELS

5.1 Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to meet the service levels in
this Section 5.

5.2 Provider shall respond to Priority 1 support requests within four (4) Business
Hours.

5.3 Provider shall maintain availability of the Services of at least 99.5% measured
monthly, corresponding to no more than three (3) hours of unavailability per month.

5.4 If availability falls below the levels stated, Customer is entitled to service
credits as follows: 5% of the monthly Fee where availability is below 99.5%; 10%
where availability is below 99%; and 25% where availability is below 95%.

5.5 The aggregate service credits payable in respect of any month shall not exceed
10% of the monthly Fee.

5.6 Service credits are Customer's sole remedy for failure to meet service levels.

---

## 6. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date as that term is defined in the
prior Master Services Agreement between the parties, and continues for an initial
term of twenty-four (24) months.

6.2 The Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods
unless terminated.

6.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement on sixty (60) days' written notice.

6.4 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the
other party commits a material breach that remains uncured for fifteen (15) days.

6.5 The notice period in Section 6.3 may not be shortened or waived by either party.

6.6 Sections 1, 4, and 8 survive termination of this Agreement.

---

## 7. LIABILITY

7.1 Each party's liability under this Agreement is subject to this Section 7.

7.2 Each party's aggregate liability is limited to the total Fees paid by Customer in
the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

7.3 The limitation in Section 7.2 does not apply to liability arising from a Security
Incident caused by Provider's negligence.

7.4 The limitation in Section 7.2 applies notwithstanding any other provision of this
Agreement.

7.5 This Section 7 survives termination of this Agreement.

7.6 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

---

## 8. MISCELLANEOUS

8.1 This Agreement supersedes the prior Master Services Agreement between the
parties and takes effect on 1 January 2027.

8.2 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent.

8.3 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Washington.

8.4 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the State of
Delaware, which shall apply Delaware law to any dispute.

8.5 If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect.

8.6 Notices must be given in writing to the addresses set out in Appendix B.
