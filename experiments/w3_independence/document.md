# ACME WIDGETS — TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY (rev 3)

Effective March 1, 2026. Supersedes rev 2 (effective January 15, 2026).

## 1. SCOPE

1.1 This policy applies to all Covered Employees.

1.2 Reimbursement requires a Qualifying Receipt.

## 2. LIMITS

2.1 Daily meal reimbursement is capped at $45 per employee.

2.2 A three-day trip therefore carries a total meal cap of $145.

2.3 Lodging is capped at $210 per night.

## 3. APPROVAL

3.1 Expenses under $500 require manager approval.

3.2 Expenses of $500 or more require Finance approval.

3.3 Expenses over $500 require manager approval only.

## 4. SUBMISSION

4.1 Reports must be submitted within 30 days of trip completion.

4.2 Late reports may be accepted at the discretion of the reviewer.

4.3 Reports submitted after February 28, 2026 remain governed by rev 2.

## 5. DEFINITIONS

5.1 "Qualifying Receipt" means a receipt that qualifies under this policy.

5.2 "Covered Employee" — see Section 7.

## 6. EXCEPTIONS

6.1 International travel is governed by Appendix B.
