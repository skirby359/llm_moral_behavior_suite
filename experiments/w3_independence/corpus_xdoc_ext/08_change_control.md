# DOCUMENT 8 — CHANGE CONTROL PROCEDURE

Schedule 5 to the Master Services Agreement between Northgate Retail Group plc
("Customer") and Halvern Systems Limited ("Provider").

---

## 1. SCOPE

1.1 This Procedure governs any change to the scope, timetable, charges or
acceptance criteria of the Services.

1.2 This Procedure does not apply to routine operational activity carried out
within the agreed scope.

## 2. RAISING A CHANGE

2.1 Either party may raise a Change Request.

2.2 A Change Request shall state the change sought, the reason for it, and the
party raising it.

2.3 Provider shall acknowledge a Change Request within two (2) Business Days.

## 3. IMPACT ASSESSMENT

3.1 Provider shall produce an impact assessment within ten (10) Business Days of
acknowledging a Change Request.

3.2 The impact assessment shall state the effect on charges, timetable,
deliverables and risk.

3.3 Where Provider considers a Change Request technically infeasible it shall say
so, with reasons, in the impact assessment.

## 4. APPROVAL

4.1 A Change Request accompanied by an impact assessment is submitted to the Change
Control Board described in the Master Services Agreement, clause 17.

4.2 A Change Request is **deemed approved** if Customer does not respond to the
impact assessment within ten (10) Business Days of receiving it.

4.3 Provider's Delivery Lead may approve, without reference to Customer, any change
whose total cost impact does not exceed £35,000.

4.4 An approved change takes effect immediately on approval.

## 5. CHARGES

5.1 Where an approved change alters the charges, the revised charges apply from the
invoice next issued after approval.

5.2 Provider shall itemise change-related charges separately on each invoice.

5.3 Change-related charges are not subject to the notice requirements applying to
ordinary charge increases.

## 6. EMERGENCY CHANGES

6.1 Provider may implement an emergency change without prior approval where
necessary to preserve the security or availability of the Services.

6.2 Provider shall notify Customer of an emergency change within one (1) Business
Day of implementing it.

6.3 Provider shall submit a retrospective Change Request within five (5) Business
Days.

## 7. REJECTED CHANGES

7.1 Where a Change Request is rejected, the Services continue unchanged.

7.2 Provider may charge for the preparation of an impact assessment for a rejected
Change Request, at the rate set out in the Exit and Transition Plan.

7.3 A rejected Change Request may be resubmitted once, with amendments.

## 8. ESCALATION

8.1 A disagreement about a Change Request escalates as follows: Delivery Lead,
then Programme Director, then Chief Information Officer.

8.2 Each escalation level has five (5) Business Days to resolve the matter before
the next level is engaged.

8.3 Escalation does not suspend the timetable in clause 3.

## 9. RECORDS

9.1 Provider shall maintain a change log recording every Change Request, its
status, and its outcome.

9.2 The change log shall be reviewed at the monthly service review.

9.3 The change log shall be retained for the duration of the Agreement.
