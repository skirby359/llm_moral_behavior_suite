# DOCUMENT 9 — EXIT AND TRANSITION PLAN

Schedule 6 to the Master Services Agreement between Northgate Retail Group plc
("Customer") and Halvern Systems Limited ("Provider").

---

## 1. PURPOSE

1.1 This Plan sets out how the Services will be transitioned away from Provider on
expiry or termination of the Agreement.

1.2 This Plan applies however the Agreement ends, including termination for breach.

## 2. TRANSITION PERIOD

2.1 The transition period begins on the date notice of termination is given and ends
six (6) months after the effective date of termination.

2.2 Provider shall continue to provide the Services throughout the transition
period on the terms then in force.

2.3 Provider shall retain all Customer data for ninety (90) days following
termination in order to support transition and any subsequent data request.

## 3. TRANSITION ASSISTANCE

3.1 Provider shall provide reasonable assistance to Customer, or to Customer's
incoming supplier, in transitioning the Services.

3.2 Transition assistance is chargeable at £1,250 per person per day.

3.3 Provider shall provide an estimate of transition effort within fifteen (15)
Business Days of a request.

3.4 Customer shall approve the estimate before assistance begins.

## 4. DELIVERABLES ON EXIT

4.1 Provider shall deliver to Customer, on or before the end of the transition
period:

  (a) all Customer data, in the format used by Provider's systems;
  (b) all Deliverables, including source code and build scripts;
  (c) current operational documentation and runbooks;
  (d) a register of all third-party dependencies and their licence terms;
  (e) the outstanding change log maintained under the Change Control Procedure.

4.2 Provider shall certify in writing that the handover under clause 4.1 is
complete.

## 5. DATA RETURN AND DELETION

5.1 Provider shall return Customer data in accordance with clause 4.1(a).

5.2 Following the retention period in clause 2.3 and completion of handover,
Provider shall delete all remaining Customer data.

5.3 Provider's obligations in respect of personal data are set out in the Data
Processing Addendum, clause 14.

## 6. KNOWLEDGE TRANSFER

6.1 Provider shall make its Delivery Lead and one solution architect available for
knowledge transfer sessions during the transition period.

6.2 Provider shall deliver up to ten (10) knowledge transfer sessions at no
additional charge.

6.3 Further sessions are chargeable at the rate in clause 3.2.

## 7. EXIT MILESTONES

7.1 The exit milestones are set out in Annex 1 to this Plan.

7.2 Provider shall report progress against the milestones fortnightly.

## 8. STAFF

8.1 Nothing in this Plan transfers any Provider employee to Customer or to an
incoming supplier.

8.2 Each party shall bear its own costs arising from any employment claim relating
to transition.

## 9. TESTING THE PLAN

9.1 The parties shall review this Plan annually.

9.2 Provider shall carry out a desk-based exit rehearsal at least once in the term.

9.3 The rehearsal report shall be provided to Customer within one (1) month.

| # | Milestone | Timing |

| 3 | Knowledge transfer sessions complete | T+60 Business Days |
| 4 | Parallel running complete | T+90 Business Days |
| 5 | Handover certified under clause 4.2 | End of transition period |
