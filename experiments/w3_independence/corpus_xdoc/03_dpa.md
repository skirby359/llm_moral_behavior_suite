# DOCUMENT 3 — DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

Executed under the Master Services Agreement between Northgate Retail Group plc
("Customer", the controller) and Halvern Systems Limited ("Provider", the
processor).

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Customer Personal Data" means personal data processed by Provider on
Customer's behalf under the Agreement.

1.2 "Data Protection Law" means all law applicable to the processing of personal
data in the territories in which the Services are provided.

1.3 "Processing" has the meaning given to it in Data Protection Law.

1.4 All Customer Personal Data is Confidential Information, whether or not marked
as confidential.

1.5 "Subprocessor" means any third party engaged by Provider to process Customer
Personal Data.

1.6 "Security Incident" means any unauthorised or unlawful access to, or
acquisition, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction of, Customer Personal
Data.

## 2. ROLES

2.1 Customer is the controller and Provider is the processor in respect of Customer
Personal Data.

2.2 Provider shall process Customer Personal Data only on Customer's documented
instructions.

2.3 Provider shall inform Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes
Data Protection Law.

## 3. SCOPE OF PROCESSING

3.1 The subject matter, nature and purpose of the processing are the provision of
the Services.

3.2 The categories of data subject are Customer's employees and retail customers.

3.3 The categories of personal data are contact details, transaction records and
loyalty scheme identifiers.

3.4 Annex 1 sets out the processing details in full.

## 4. LOCATION OF PROCESSING

4.1 Provider shall process Customer Personal Data only within the United Kingdom
and the European Economic Area.

4.2 Provider shall not transfer Customer Personal Data outside those territories
without Customer's prior written authorisation and an appropriate transfer
mechanism.

4.3 Annex 2 lists the approved processing locations.

## 5. SUBPROCESSORS

5.1 Provider shall not engage any Subprocessor without Customer's prior written
consent to that specific Subprocessor.

5.2 Provider shall impose on each Subprocessor obligations equivalent to those in
this Addendum.

5.3 Provider remains liable for the acts and omissions of each Subprocessor.

## 6. SECURITY INCIDENTS

6.1 Provider shall notify Customer of any Security Incident without undue delay and
in any event within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of it.

6.2 The notification shall describe the nature of the Security Incident, the
categories and approximate number of data subjects affected, and the measures
taken or proposed.

6.3 Provider shall not make any public statement about a Security Incident
affecting Customer Personal Data without Customer's prior written approval.

## 7. SECURITY MEASURES

7.1 Provider shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to
protect Customer Personal Data.

7.2 Those measures include encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access
control, and logging of access to Customer Personal Data.

7.3 Provider shall review those measures at least annually.

## 8. RETURN AND DELETION

8.1 On Customer's request during the term, Provider shall return a copy of Customer
Personal Data in a structured, commonly used format.

8.2 On expiry or termination of the Agreement, Provider shall delete all Customer
Personal Data in its possession or control within thirty (30) days.

8.3 Provider shall certify that deletion in writing.

## 9. AUDIT

9.1 Customer may audit Provider's compliance with this Addendum once in any twelve
(12) month period, on not less than fourteen (14) days' written notice.

9.2 An audit may include inspection of Provider's premises, systems and records
relating to Customer Personal Data.

9.3 Each party bears its own costs of an audit.

## 10. ASSISTANCE

10.1 Provider shall assist Customer in responding to requests from data subjects.

10.2 Provider shall assist Customer with data protection impact assessments and
with consultations with supervisory authorities.

## 11. LIABILITY

11.1 No limitation or exclusion of liability in the Agreement applies to any breach
of this Addendum or of Data Protection Law.

11.2 Each party shall indemnify the other against fines imposed on it as a result
of the other's breach of Data Protection Law.

## 12. PRECEDENCE

12.1 This Addendum forms part of the Agreement.

## 13. GOVERNING LAW

13.1 This Addendum is governed by the law applicable to the Agreement, save as
provided in clause 13.2.

13.2 This Addendum and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of
the Republic of Ireland.
