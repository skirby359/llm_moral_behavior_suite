# DOCUMENT 4 — SERVICE LEVEL AND SUPPORT SCHEDULE

Schedule 2 to the Master Services Agreement between Northgate Retail Group plc
("Customer") and Halvern Systems Limited ("Provider").

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Business Day" means Monday to Saturday, excluding US federal holidays.

1.2 "Incident" means an unplanned interruption to, or reduction in the quality of,
the Services.

1.3 "Priority 1" means an Incident which renders the platform wholly unavailable to
all users.

1.4 "Priority 2" means an Incident which materially degrades the platform for a
substantial proportion of users.

1.5 "Priority 3" means any other Incident.

1.6 "Resolution" means the restoration of the Services to normal operation,
including by means of a documented workaround.

## 2. SUPPORT HOURS

2.1 Priority 1 support is available twenty-four hours a day, every day.

2.2 Priority 2 and Priority 3 support is available from 08:00 to 18:00 on Business
Days.

2.3 Support is provided in English.

## 3. AVAILABILITY

3.1 Provider shall achieve monthly availability of the platform of not less than
99.9%.

3.2 Availability is calculated as (total minutes in the month less unavailable
minutes) divided by total minutes in the month.

3.3 Agreed maintenance windows are excluded from the calculation.

3.4 Provider shall give not less than five (5) Business Days' notice of a
maintenance window.

## 4. SERVICE CREDITS

4.1 Where Provider fails to achieve the availability level in clause 3.1, Customer
becomes entitled to a service credit, which Provider shall apply automatically to
the next invoice without any requirement for Customer to claim it.

4.2 Service credits in respect of any month shall not exceed ten per cent (10%) of
the monthly Fee for that month.

4.3 Service credits are Customer's sole financial remedy for failure to meet the
availability level.

## 5. RESPONSE AND RESOLUTION TARGETS

5.1 Priority 1 — response within fifteen (15) minutes; Resolution within four (4)
hours.

5.2 Priority 2 — response within two (2) hours; Resolution within one (1) Business
Day.

5.3 Priority 3 — response within one (1) Business Day; Resolution within five (5)
Business Days.

5.4 Response and Resolution times are measured from the point at which Provider
acknowledges the Incident in its service management tool.

## 6. REPORTING

6.1 Provider shall publish a monthly service report within ten (10) Business Days
of the end of each month.

6.2 The report shall state achieved availability, Incident volumes by priority, and
performance against the targets in clause 5.

6.3 Provider shall retain service reports for the duration of the Agreement.

## 7. SECURITY INCIDENTS

7.1 "Security Incident" means confirmed unauthorised access to Customer data
resulting in loss or corruption of that data.

7.2 A Security Incident is treated as a Priority 1 Incident for the purposes of
clause 5.1.

7.3 Provider shall notify Customer's nominated security contact of a Security
Incident within seventy-two (72) hours of confirming it.

7.4 The escalation contacts for Security Incidents are listed in Annex 3 of the
Data Processing Addendum.

## 8. CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

8.1 Provider shall carry out a root cause analysis for every Priority 1 Incident
and provide it to Customer within five (5) Business Days of Resolution.

8.2 Provider shall maintain a register of open corrective actions.

8.3 The parties shall review the register at the monthly service review.

## 9. EXCLUSIONS

9.1 Provider is not responsible for unavailability caused by Customer's own network
or equipment.

9.2 Provider is not responsible for unavailability caused by a third-party service
outside Provider's control, provided Provider has taken reasonable steps to
mitigate it.

9.3 Provider is not responsible for unavailability during an agreed maintenance
window.
