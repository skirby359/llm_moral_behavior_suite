# DOCUMENT 2 — STATEMENT OF WORK 1 (SCHEDULE 1)

Issued under the Master Services Agreement between Northgate Retail Group plc
("Customer") and Halvern Systems Limited ("Provider").

---

## 1. STATUS OF THIS DOCUMENT

1.1 This Statement of Work is Schedule 1 to the Master Services Agreement.

1.2 In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between this Statement of Work
and the Master Services Agreement, this Statement of Work prevails.

1.3 Terms defined in the Master Services Agreement have the same meaning here
unless otherwise stated.

## 2. PROGRAMME AND DURATION

2.1 Provider shall deliver a thirty-six (36) month replatforming programme.

2.2 The programme commences on 1 March 2027 and proceeds in the four phases set
out in section 4.

2.3 Either party may terminate this Statement of Work for convenience by giving not
less than thirty (30) days' written notice.

2.4 Termination of this Statement of Work does not of itself terminate the Master
Services Agreement.

## 3. TEAM AND LOCATION

3.1 Provider shall assign a delivery team of not fewer than eleven (11) full-time
equivalent personnel.

3.2 The team comprises one delivery lead, two solution architects, six engineers,
one test lead and one business analyst.

3.3 The delivery lead shall be available during Customer's working hours.

3.4 The delivery team operates from Provider's development centre in Bengaluru,
India.

3.5 Provider shall not substitute named key personnel without Customer's prior
written agreement.

## 4. PHASES

4.1 Phase 1 — Discovery and current-state assessment. Duration three (3) months.

4.2 Phase 2 — Platform build and integration. Duration fifteen (15) months.

4.3 Phase 3 — Migration and parallel running. Duration twelve (12) months.

4.4 Phase 4 — Stabilisation and handover. Duration six (6) months.

4.5 Phases may overlap only where agreed in writing.

## 5. CHARGES

5.1 The monthly Fee for the Services described in this Statement of Work is
£12,800.

5.2 The Fee covers all personnel, tooling and travel unless expressly stated
otherwise.

5.3 Provider shall invoice monthly in arrears. Customer shall pay each undisputed
invoice within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

5.4 Expenses outside the Fee require Customer's prior written approval and are
reimbursed at cost.

## 6. DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

6.1 The Deliverables for each phase are listed in the phase plan maintained by the
delivery lead.

6.2 Provider shall submit each Deliverable to Customer's nominated contact for
review.

6.3 A Deliverable is deemed accepted if Customer does not respond within five (5)
Business Days of submission.

6.4 Where Customer responds with comments, Provider shall address them and
resubmit within ten (10) Business Days.

## 7. SERVICE LEVELS FOR THE PLATFORM

7.1 From the start of Phase 3, the platform is subject to service levels.

7.2 Provider shall achieve monthly availability of not less than 99.5%.

7.3 Availability is measured across each calendar month, excluding agreed
maintenance windows.

7.4 Where availability falls below the level in section 7.2, Customer is entitled
to service credits of up to twenty-five per cent (25%) of the monthly Fee.

## 8. GOVERNANCE

8.1 The parties shall hold a monthly service review.

8.2 The parties shall hold a quarterly steering committee attended by an executive
sponsor from each party.

8.3 Escalation follows the path: delivery lead, then account director, then
executive sponsor.

## 9. COMMERCIAL PROTECTIONS

9.1 Provider shall not solicit Customer's personnel during the programme.

9.2 Provider's total aggregate liability under this Statement of Work shall not
exceed £250,000.

9.3 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

9.4 Provider shall maintain professional indemnity insurance with a limit of
indemnity of not less than £2,000,000 in the aggregate.

## 10. ASSUMPTIONS

10.1 Customer will make subject-matter experts available within five (5) Business
Days of a written request.

10.2 Customer's existing integration documentation is accurate and current.

10.3 Provider has assumed no more than three (3) legacy interfaces require
bespoke adapters.
