# DOCUMENT 1 — MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Parties:** Northgate Retail Group plc ("Customer") and Halvern Systems Limited
("Provider").

**Effective Date:** 1 April 2027.

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Agreement" means this Master Services Agreement together with each Schedule
and Addendum executed under it.

1.2 "Business Day" means Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in England
and Wales.

1.3 "Deliverable" means any item identified as a deliverable in a Statement of
Work.

1.4 "Fee" means the amounts payable by Customer under clause 4.

1.5 "Services" means the services described in a Statement of Work.

1.6 "Schedule" means a document executed by both parties and expressly stated to
be a Schedule to this Agreement.

## 2. STRUCTURE

2.1 The Services shall be performed in accordance with one or more Statements of
Work. Statement of Work 1 is designated Schedule 1.

2.2 Provider shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care and in
accordance with good industry practice.

2.3 Provider shall comply with Customer's reasonable instructions as to the manner
of performance of the Services.

2.4 Any change to the Services shall be agreed in writing by both parties before
being implemented.

## 3. TERM

3.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial
term of twenty-four (24) months.

3.2 Following the initial term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive
periods of twelve (12) months unless terminated in accordance with clause 3.3.

3.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving not less
than ninety (90) days' written notice to the other party.

3.4 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the
other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy that breach within
fifteen (15) days of written notice requiring it to do so.

## 4. CHARGES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Customer shall pay Provider a monthly Fee of £12,400 for the Services.

4.2 The Fee is exclusive of value added tax, which shall be added to each invoice
at the prevailing rate.

4.3 Provider shall invoice monthly in arrears. Customer shall pay each undisputed
invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

4.4 Provider may increase the Fee once in any twelve (12) month period by giving
not less than sixty (60) days' written notice, provided the increase does not
exceed the annual change in the Consumer Prices Index.

4.5 Customer shall pay all amounts due under this Agreement in full without any
set-off, counterclaim, deduction or withholding, except as required by law or as
agreed by Provider in writing.

## 5. CUSTOMER OBLIGATIONS

5.1 Customer shall provide Provider with such access to its systems, premises and
personnel as Provider reasonably requires to perform the Services.

5.2 Customer shall nominate a single point of contact for the Services and shall
notify Provider of any change to that nomination.

## 6. ACCEPTANCE

6.1 No Deliverable shall be treated as accepted unless and until Customer has
confirmed its acceptance expressly and in writing.

6.2 Customer shall assess each Deliverable against the acceptance criteria set out
in Schedule 1, Section 12.

6.3 Where Customer rejects a Deliverable, Provider shall correct it and resubmit
it at no additional charge.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

7.1 Customer retains all right, title and interest in Customer materials and data.

7.2 Provider assigns to Customer all intellectual property rights in the
Deliverables on payment in full for them.

7.3 Provider retains all right, title and interest in its pre-existing tools,
methods and know-how.

## 8. WARRANTIES

8.1 Each party warrants that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement.

8.2 Provider warrants that the Deliverables will conform to the applicable
acceptance criteria for a period of ninety (90) days following acceptance.

## 9. SUBCONTRACTING

9.1 Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors
as if they were its own.

9.2 Provider shall ensure each subcontractor is bound by obligations no less
onerous than those in this Agreement.

9.3 Provider may subcontract the performance of any part of the Services without
the prior consent of Customer.

## 10. AUDIT AND ASSURANCE

10.1 Provider shall maintain records sufficient to demonstrate its compliance with
this Agreement.

10.2 Provider shall furnish Customer with its annual SOC 2 Type II report. Customer
shall have no further right to audit, inspect or attend Provider's premises or
systems.

## 11. CONFIDENTIALITY

11.1 "Confidential Information" means information disclosed by one party to the
other which is marked as confidential in writing at the time of disclosure.

11.2 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information secret and shall
not disclose it to any third party except to its personnel who need to know it.

11.3 The obligations in clause 11.2 continue for three (3) years after expiry or
termination of this Agreement.

## 12. RECORDS AND RETENTION

12.1 Each party shall keep accurate records of its performance under this
Agreement.

12.2 Provider shall make its records available to Customer on reasonable request
in accordance with clause 10.2.

12.3 Records shall be kept in a form capable of being reproduced legibly.

12.4 Provider shall retain all records, data and materials relating to this
Agreement for a period of seven (7) years following expiry or termination.

## 13. LIABILITY

13.1 Subject to clause 13.2, each party's total aggregate liability arising out of
or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the total Fees paid or
payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

13.2 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury
caused by negligence, or for fraud.

13.3 The limit in clause 13.1 applies notwithstanding any other provision of any
Schedule or Addendum.

## 14. ORDER OF PRECEDENCE

14.1 This Agreement and each Schedule and Addendum are to be read together as one
agreement.

14.2 In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between this Agreement and any
Schedule or Addendum, this Agreement prevails.

## 15. GOVERNING LAW

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of
England and Wales.

15.2 The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 16. INSURANCE

16.1 Provider shall maintain professional indemnity insurance with a limit of
indemnity of not less than £5,000,000 in the aggregate.

16.2 Provider shall produce evidence of that insurance on Customer's written
request.
