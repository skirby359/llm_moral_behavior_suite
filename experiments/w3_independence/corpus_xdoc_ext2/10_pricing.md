# DOCUMENT 10 — PRICING AND RATE CARD

Schedule 7 to the Master Services Agreement between Northgate Retail Group plc
("Customer") and Halvern Systems Limited ("Provider").

---

## 1. RECURRING CHARGES

1.1 The recurring monthly charge for the platform is £13,100, comprising a base
platform charge and a managed-service surcharge.

1.2 The recurring charge is invoiced monthly in advance.

1.3 The recurring charge is fixed for the initial term and is not subject to
indexation or any other increase during that period.

1.4 All charges are stated in GBP and exclude value added tax.

## 2. STANDARD RATE CARD

2.1 Time-and-materials work outside the recurring charge is billed at the standard
rate of £1,450 per person per day.

2.2 A working day is seven and a half (7.5) hours.

2.3 Part days are billed in half-day increments.

2.4 The standard rate applies to every role unless a role-specific rate is set out
in Exhibit B.

## 3. EXPENSES

3.1 Travel and subsistence are recoverable at cost against receipts.

3.2 Expenses require prior written approval where the total exceeds £500 in any
month.

3.3 Mileage is reimbursed at 45p per mile.

## 4. SERVICE CREDITS

4.1 Service credits arising under any Schedule are applied as a deduction from the
next recurring invoice.

4.2 The aggregate of all service credits in any month shall not exceed five per
cent (5%) of the recurring monthly charge for that month.

4.3 Service credits do not carry forward between months.

## 5. VOLUME BANDS

5.1 The recurring charge assumes up to 250,000 transactions per calendar month.

5.2 Volumes above that band are charged at £0.004 per transaction.

5.3 Provider shall report actual volumes in the monthly service report.

## 6. INVOICING

6.1 Invoices are issued on the first Business Day of each month.

6.2 Each invoice shall itemise the recurring charge, any time-and-materials work,
any expenses, and any service credits applied.

6.3 Queries on an invoice must be raised within ten (10) Business Days of receipt.

## 7. REVIEW

7.1 The parties shall review this rate card at each anniversary of the Service
Commencement Date.

7.2 Any change to this rate card is a change for the purposes of the Change Control
Procedure.
