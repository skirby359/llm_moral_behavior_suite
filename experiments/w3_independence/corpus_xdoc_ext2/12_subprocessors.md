# DOCUMENT 12 — APPROVED SUBPROCESSOR LIST

Schedule 9 to the Master Services Agreement between Northgate Retail Group plc
("Customer") and Halvern Systems Limited ("Provider").

This Schedule is Annex 2 to the Data Processing Addendum.

---

## 1. STATUS

1.1 This list records the third parties engaged by Provider to process Customer
Personal Data.

1.2 Provider maintains this list and publishes the current version on its trust
portal.

## 3. CHANGES TO THIS LIST

3.1 Provider may add or replace a Subprocessor by giving Customer thirty (30) days'
written notice.

3.2 Customer has a right to object to a new Subprocessor within that notice period,
on reasonable grounds relating to data protection.

3.3 Where Customer objects, the parties shall discuss in good faith. If no
resolution is reached, Provider may proceed and Customer may terminate the affected
Services on thirty (30) days' notice.

3.4 Provider may replace a Subprocessor immediately, with notice afterwards, where
necessary to maintain the security or continuity of the Services.

## 4. FLOW-DOWN OBLIGATIONS

4.1 Provider shall impose on each Subprocessor written obligations equivalent to
those in the Data Processing Addendum.

4.2 Provider shall carry out due diligence on each Subprocessor before engagement
and at least annually thereafter.

4.3 Provider remains liable to Customer for each Subprocessor's performance.

## 5. TRANSFERS

5.1 Where a Subprocessor is located outside the United Kingdom, Provider shall put
in place an appropriate transfer mechanism.

5.2 Provider shall make the relevant transfer documentation available to Customer on
request.

## 6. REVIEW

6.1 The parties shall review this list at the quarterly steering committee.

6.2 Provider shall confirm in writing at each review that the list is complete and
current.
