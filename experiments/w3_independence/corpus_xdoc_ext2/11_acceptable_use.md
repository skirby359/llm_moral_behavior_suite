# DOCUMENT 11 — ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

Schedule 8 to the Master Services Agreement between Northgate Retail Group plc
("Customer") and Halvern Systems Limited ("Provider").

---

## 1. SCOPE

1.1 This Policy governs Customer's use of the platform and the content Customer
places on it.

1.2 Customer is responsible for the acts and omissions of its authorised users.

## 2. PERMITTED USE

2.1 The platform may be used only for Customer's internal retail operations and for
serving Customer's retail customers.

2.2 Customer shall not resell or provide the platform to any third party.

2.3 Customer shall not use the platform to develop a competing service.

## 3. PROHIBITED CONTENT

3.1 Customer shall not store on the platform any personal data relating to a person
under 16 years of age.

3.2 Customer shall not store payment card primary account numbers on the platform.

3.3 Customer shall not store any unlawful, defamatory or infringing content.

## 4. MONITORING

4.1 Provider does not monitor the content Customer places on the platform and
accepts no responsibility for it.

4.2 Provider may inspect content only where it has a reasonable belief that this
Policy has been breached, or where required by law.

4.3 Customer shall report any suspected misuse of the platform to Provider promptly.

## 5. SUSPENSION

5.1 Provider may suspend the Services immediately and without prior notice where it
reasonably believes this Policy has been breached.

5.2 Provider shall notify Customer of a suspension as soon as practicable
afterwards.

5.3 Provider shall restore the Services once the breach is remedied to Provider's
reasonable satisfaction.

5.4 Charges continue to accrue during a suspension under this clause.

## 6. LAWFUL DISCLOSURE

6.1 Provider may disclose Customer content to a law enforcement or regulatory body
where Provider considers it is required to do so.

6.2 Provider is not obliged to notify Customer before or after making such a
disclosure.

## 7. SECURITY OF CREDENTIALS

7.1 Customer shall keep authentication credentials confidential.

7.2 Customer shall notify Provider immediately on becoming aware of a compromised
credential.

7.3 Customer is responsible for all activity carried out using its credentials.

## 8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

8.1 Provider may amend this Policy on thirty (30) days' notice.

8.2 Continued use of the platform after that period constitutes acceptance of the
amended Policy.

## 9. CONSEQUENCES OF BREACH

9.1 A material breach of this Policy is a material breach of the Agreement.

9.2 Provider may recover from Customer any costs reasonably incurred as a result of
a breach of this Policy.
