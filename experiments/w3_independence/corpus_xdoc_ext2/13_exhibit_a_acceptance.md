# DOCUMENT 13 — EXHIBIT A: DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

Exhibit A to Statement of Work 1 between Northgate Retail Group plc ("Customer") and
Halvern Systems Limited ("Provider").

---

## 1. STATUS

1.1 This Exhibit sets out the Deliverables and the criteria against which each is
assessed.

1.2 This Exhibit is the sole source of the acceptance criteria for the programme.

## 2. DELIVERABLES

| # | Deliverable | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Current-state assessment report | 1 |
| D2 | Target architecture specification | 1 |
| D3 | Integrated platform build | 2 |
| D4 | Data migration toolset | 3 |
| D5 | Migrated production dataset | 3 |
| D6 | Operational runbooks | 4 |
| D7 | Handover and training pack | 4 |

## 3. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

3.1 D1 is accepted when it covers every in-scope system and is signed off by
Customer's Director of Digital Platforms.

3.2 D2 is accepted when it addresses every requirement in the requirements register
and passes an architecture review.

3.3 D3 is accepted when it passes the agreed functional test pack with no Material
Defect outstanding.

3.4 D4 is accepted when it migrates a representative sample with zero record loss.

3.5 D5 is accepted when reconciliation shows a variance of not more than 0.01%.

3.6 D6 is accepted when Customer's operations team can execute each runbook
unaided.

3.7 D7 is accepted when training has been delivered to at least twenty (20) named
staff.

## 4. DEFINITIONS

4.1 "Material Defect" means a defect that prevents a Deliverable being used for its
intended purpose.

4.2 "Minor Defect" means any other defect.

4.3 A Deliverable may be accepted with Minor Defects outstanding, provided Provider
commits to a remediation date.

## 5. ACCEPTANCE PROCESS

5.1 Provider submits a Deliverable with a completion statement.

5.2 Customer has fifteen (15) Business Days to assess the Deliverable and issue
either an acceptance certificate or a written statement of defects.

5.3 Acceptance requires a signed acceptance certificate. Silence does not
constitute acceptance.

5.4 Where Customer issues a statement of defects, Provider shall remedy and
resubmit, and a further fifteen (15) Business Day assessment period applies.

## 6. DISPUTES

6.1 A dispute about whether a Deliverable meets its criteria is escalated under the
governance provisions of the Statement of Work.

6.2 Pending resolution, Provider shall continue to perform.

## 7. WARRANTY

7.1 Each accepted Deliverable is warranted against Material Defects for one hundred
and eighty (180) days from acceptance.
