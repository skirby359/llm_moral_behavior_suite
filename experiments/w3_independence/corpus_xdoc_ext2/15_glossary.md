# DOCUMENT 15 — GLOSSARY OF DEFINED TERMS

Schedule 10 to the Master Services Agreement between Northgate Retail Group plc
("Customer") and Halvern Systems Limited ("Provider").

---

## 1. STATUS OF THIS GLOSSARY

1.1 This Glossary collects the terms used across the contract documents.

1.2 The definitions in this Glossary prevail over any definition of the same term
appearing in any other document.

1.3 A term not defined here has the meaning given to it where it is used.

## 2. DEFINED TERMS

**Agreement** — the Master Services Agreement, the Order Form, and every Schedule,
Exhibit and Addendum entered into under any of them.

**Business Day** — Monday to Friday, including public holidays.

**Confidential Information** — any information disclosed by one party to the other
in connection with the Agreement, whether or not it is marked as confidential and
whether disclosed orally or in writing.

**Change Request** — a request to alter the Services, raised under the Change
Control Procedure.

**Deliverable** — any output of the Services provided to Customer, whether or not
identified as a deliverable in a Statement of Work.

**Fee** — all amounts payable by Customer under the Agreement, including recurring
charges, time-and-materials charges and expenses.

**Key Personnel** — the roles identified in Exhibit B.

**Material Defect** — a defect that prevents a Deliverable being used for its
intended purpose.

**Personal Data** — personal data as defined in the UK General Data Protection
Regulation.

**Restricted Data** — data classified as Restricted under the Information Security
Policy.

**Security Incident** — any event affecting the confidentiality, integrity or
availability of Customer data or of the platform.

**Services** — the services described in a Statement of Work, together with the
support services described in the Service Level and Support Schedule.

**Subprocessor** — a third party engaged by Provider to process Personal Data.

**Transition Period** — the period defined in the Exit and Transition Plan.

## 3. INTERPRETATION

3.1 Headings are for convenience and do not affect interpretation.

3.2 The singular includes the plural and vice versa.

3.3 A reference to a document is to that document as amended from time to time.

3.4 "Including" means including without limitation.

3.5 A reference to a statute includes any subordinate legislation made under it.

## 4. MAINTENANCE

4.1 This Glossary is maintained by Provider's Commercial Director.

4.2 Any addition to this Glossary is a change for the purposes of the Change
Control Procedure.
