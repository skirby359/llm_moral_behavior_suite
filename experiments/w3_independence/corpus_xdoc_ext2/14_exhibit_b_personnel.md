# DOCUMENT 14 — EXHIBIT B: KEY PERSONNEL AND ROLE RATES

Exhibit B to Statement of Work 1 between Northgate Retail Group plc ("Customer") and
Halvern Systems Limited ("Provider").

---

## 1. KEY PERSONNEL

| Role | Commitment | Base location |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery Lead | Full time | London, United Kingdom |
| Lead Solution Architect | Full time | London, United Kingdom |
| Second Solution Architect | Full time | Bengaluru, India |
| Test Lead | Full time | Bengaluru, India |

1.1 The individuals holding these roles at the Service Commencement Date are named
in the programme initiation document.

1.2 Key Personnel are dedicated to this programme and shall not be assigned to
other Provider engagements.

## 2. SUBSTITUTION

2.1 Provider may substitute a member of Key Personnel by giving Customer ten (10)
Business Days' written notice.

2.2 A substitute shall be of equivalent skill and experience.

2.3 Provider shall fund a two (2) week handover overlap at its own cost.

2.4 Provider may substitute immediately where a person resigns, is dismissed, or is
absent for medical reasons.

## 3. ROLE RATES

| Role | Day rate |
|---|---|
| Delivery Lead | £1,450 |
| Solution Architect | £1,450 |
| Engineer | £1,100 |
| Test Lead | £1,100 |
| Business Analyst | £950 |

3.1 These rates apply to time-and-materials work in place of the standard rate.

3.2 Rates are held for the initial term and reviewed annually thereafter.

## 4. NON-KEY PERSONNEL

4.1 Provider may assign, reassign and replace non-key personnel at its discretion.

4.2 Provider shall maintain the team size committed in the Statement of Work.

## 5. VETTING

5.1 All personnel are vetted in accordance with the Information Security Policy.

5.2 Provider shall provide evidence of vetting for Key Personnel on request.

## 6. CUSTOMER CONCERNS

6.1 Customer may request removal of any individual whose performance or conduct is
unsatisfactory, acting reasonably.

6.2 Provider shall replace that individual within twenty (20) Business Days.
